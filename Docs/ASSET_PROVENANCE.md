# Asset provenance

The interface, generic EFI mark, Windows-style four-pane compatibility glyph,
Linux shield, cyber frame, animation, and renderer are implemented as native
APEX32 drawing code.

The Kali and BlackArch monochrome masks are compatibility identifiers derived
for the personal hardware prototype. Before a stable public release, every
third-party mark must be reviewed against its official trademark and asset-use
policy. Names and marks belong to their respective owners; inclusion does not
imply endorsement.
